# ADR 0045 — Base is the preferred launch chain

**Date:** 2026-10-02. **Status:** accepted chain preference, recorded from Josh; platform and pairing open.
**Reasoning:** [design 0035](../design/0035-base-launch-selection.md).
**Comparison:** [research 0067](../research/0067-base-launch-platform-comparison.md).

Josh says Base is the preferred launch chain and asks us to compare launchpads.
Base replaces Solana/pump.fun/SOL as the working token launch target. Flaunch
and Clanker are candidates; no launchpad, pairing or allocation is selected.
Flaunch-first qualification is a recommendation, not an owner decision.

This amends only the token chain/platform/pair selection in
[ADR 0037](0037-the-token-launches-on-pump-fun-and-its-fees-pay-for-operations.md),
[ADR 0043](0043-the-public-library-and-compounding-intelligence.md) decisions 2/6,
and [ADR 0044](0044-three-chain-investigations-and-shared-cases.md)'s retained
Solana launch sentence. Preserve their dated decisions and existing readers.
Solana, Base and Ethereum investigation coverage remains required.

Keep the joint investigator/token launch, free Library, later paid portal,
equal evidence standards, startup maximum of $200 and current spending limits.
Model judgment has no financial authority. Treasury spending remains manual;
the proposed burn/liquidity split and protocol-native automation need a separate
configuration decision, control/cost review and execution evidence.

Before launch, choose the platform and pairing from measured deployment and
funding evidence, implement its specific readback and reconciled ledger, and
replace the historical Solana runbook with a qualified Base procedure. The
existing quality, review, X approval and owner-signing gates remain. This ADR
does not launch, deploy, sign, spend or move any token.
