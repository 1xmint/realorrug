# ADR 0043 — the public Library and Compounding Intelligence

**Date:** 2026-09-30
**Status:** accepted. Josh's approved documentation-refresh plan, recorded.
**Reasoning:** [design 0033](../design/0033-the-library-and-connected-flywheels.md).
**Canonical direction:** [VISION.md](../../VISION.md).
**Delivery order:** [ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md).

## Decision

**Partially amended 2026-10-02 by [ADR 0045](0045-base-is-the-preferred-launch-chain.md):**
the Solana/pump.fun/SOL choice in decisions 2/6 is replaced by a preferred
Base launch with launchpad and pairing open. The original decisions below
remain dated history; other product, funding and evidence rules remain.

1. VISION is the source of truth for product intent; ROADMAP derives delivery
   order and acceptance evidence. AGENTS governs engineering and evidence.
   Earlier intent and plan documents remain history with explicit pointers.
   A vision describes intended behavior; code/captures establish current behavior.
2. Launch the investigator and Solana/pump.fun token together with a free
   public Library of living, collaborative token dossiers. Preserve questions,
   sources, observation times, gaps, corrections and assessment history.
3. Label five connected flywheels: Attention and Participation, Compounding
   Intelligence, Sustainable Funding, Token Participation and Trust. Each
   has dependencies, failure points, measurements and implementation status.
   Compounding Intelligence includes verified relationships, mechanisms and
   legitimate lookalikes, outcomes, playbooks and evaluation cases actually
   used by subsequent investigations.
4. Paid investigations and a portal follow the joint launch. Start with one
   bounded option; add depth tiers and explicit-list batches after cost and
   capacity measurement. Paid work enriches public cases and never changes
   evidence standards. No tier price or new payment rail is adopted here.
5. Josh's startup contribution is $100–$200, capped at $200 total, using free
   options first, with no implied recurring commitment. Retain current runtime
   ceilings until explicit implementation changes them. Received revenue
   cannot silently increase a budget; customer-funded expansion requires its
   own admission, fulfillment and cost accounting.
6. Keep Solana/pump.fun with SOL pairing as selected by ADR 0037. Compare
   alternatives and disclose actual fee, liquidity, custody and program powers.
   Verify the selected configuration and terms before launch; documentation
   about final fee shares does not prove our deployed controls.
7. Reopen buy-and-burn and permanent-liquidity allocations for evaluation.
   The 25% / 25% example is proposed, not an adopted allocation or public
   promise. Resolve denominator, operating coverage, reserve cap/excess rules,
   costs and enforceable execution/control safeguards before adopting it.
   Launch defaults to operator-managed operations and a bounded reserve.
8. Financial records distinguish allocated, pending, executed and verified
   actions. Publish receipts, expenses, balances, runway and powers, separating
   chain verification from operator reporting. Eventual automation needs a
   separate reviewed decision and constrained execution. Model judgment never
   moves money or holds spending authority; the retired payout signer stays retired.
9. Forecasts and reputation are preserved outside the critical launch path.
   Reply quality, the existing launch review, X approval and owner signing
   remain launch gates. This decision changes no code, limits, credentials,
   deployment, signing or live financial behavior.

## Precise amendments and retained decisions

- **ADR 0036:** its facts-only/Base USDC endpoint remains the implementation
  record. It does not determine future investigation scopes/prices or prove
  payment readiness. The portal follows launch and requires recovery gates.
- **ADR 0037 decision 2 and ADR 0038 decision 2:** operations/reserve remains
  the current launch default. Their permanent exclusion of evaluating
  buybacks is superseded by decision 7, not by an accepted buyback program.
  No staking reward, revenue share, yield, prize or airdrop is adopted.
- **ADR 0037 decision 3:** retained for current operation. Future separately
  constrained automation requires its own decision; no model obtains a key or
  discretionary money-movement authority through this ADR.
- **ADR 0039 decision 5:** replace the $90 monthly figure as the owner's
  enduring funding commitment with the initial $200 maximum in decision 5.
  Its implemented monthly enforcement remains intact pending a reviewed
  change; caching, admission limits and missing-budget refusal remain required.
- **Plan 0002 / INTENT's 2026-09-26 phase-4 prerequisite:** replaced by
  Library and claim-investigation launch requirements. Forecasts/reputation
  are no longer prerequisites. Earlier entries remain dated history.
- Keep ADR 0037's chain/pair choice, wallet and compensation disclosure,
  actual launch readback and owner signing; ADR 0038's equal evidence standards,
  no valuable prizes, no vote-determined facts and non-financial reputation;
  ADR 0039's quality/X/launch gates, amended by ADR 0042's review process.

## Consequences

The frontend becomes a public case library as well as an X companion. The
agent must preserve claims, perform bounded research and retrieve relevant
case intelligence; cached dossiers alone do not close F2. Treasury transparency
and future paid-work fulfillment need durable records rather than claims of
automatic execution. New economics remain gated proposals. Product and launch
copy must match implemented behavior before it is published.
